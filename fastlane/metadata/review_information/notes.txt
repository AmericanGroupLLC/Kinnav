Thanks for reviewing Kinnav.

Kinnav is a personal safety and wellbeing app for women: a map of guardians, a
one-tap Safe Call, confirmed emergency dialling, safety contacts, self-care
modules and rewards.

SIGN-IN ACCOUNT
The Sign Up screen has a "Use test account" button that signs in with one tap.
If you prefer to type them, the credentials are:

  Email:    qa@safecodeg.com
  Password: QATest@2026!

Authentication is Supabase email/password.

TEST INSTRUCTIONS
1. Launch the app and step through onboarding.
2. On the Sign Up screen tap "Use test account".
3. Complete the short profile step (name and age; the app enforces 18+).
4. You land on the guardian map. Allow location when prompted — the map centres
   on you and shows nearby guardians.
5. Tap CALL GUARDIANS to open the call options: Voice, Video, Text, Emergency.
6. Tap Voice or Video to see the Safe Call screen, or Text to open chat.
7. Open the drawer (top-left) for Profile, Safety Contacts, Call History,
   Modules, Rewards, Subscription, About and Legal.

PLEASE NOTE — WHAT IS AND IS NOT LIVE
We want to be explicit rather than have you discover it mid-review.

• The guardian network is not live yet. The guardians on the map are sample
  profiles.
• Safe Call is a simulation. The Safe Call screen states this on screen
  ("DEMO - simulated safe call"); it does not connect to a live responder.
• Emergency dialling is real. It asks for confirmation and then dials 911.
  Please do not confirm that dialog during review.
• Subscription screen is presentational in this build. No StoreKit products are
  configured and no purchase can be completed.

Everything else — account, profile, map, location, safety contacts, modules,
progress, rewards and call history — is fully functional.

NOT AN EMERGENCY SERVICE
Kinnav is not a medical or emergency service and is not a replacement for one.
The app and our Terms both say so, and the app routes users to 911 for real
emergencies.

PERMISSIONS
• Location (when in use): to place the user and nearby guardians on the map.
• Camera: for a video Safe Call.

TRACKING
No advertising or analytics SDK ships in this build and the app does not access
the IDFA, so no App Tracking Transparency prompt appears. This matches the
"does not use IDFA" answer in the submission.
